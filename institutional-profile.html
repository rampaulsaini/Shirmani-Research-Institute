3. Quality and delivery principles

Scope before work

Deliverable, language, source material, price, deadline and revision count are agreed in writing.

Evidence-led research

When research is included, distinguish sourced facts, interpretation, uncertainty and missing evidence.

Human review

Work is checked against the agreed brief before delivery. No unsupported accuracy or certification claims.

Privacy by restraint

Do not send passwords, OTPs, bank credentials, patient/child data or confidential institutional records through public enquiry channels.

Fair commercial terms

No hidden renewal, forced purchase, fabricated scarcity, invented testimonials or unlimited free work.

Corrections

One consolidated revision is included in the pilot. Any further work is quoted and approved separately.

4. Contact and partnership policy

  • Use only official, publicly published business or procurement channels and respect each institution's stated contact rules.
  • No bulk unsolicited email, personal-data scraping, guessed addresses, repeated follow-ups after refusal, or bypassing tender Q&A restrictions.
  • For procurement, respond only through the prescribed portal and only when eligibility, staffing, quality, security and legal requirements can be evidenced.
  • First exchange should establish the institution's need, current workflow, acceptance criteria, budget/procurement route and data constraints before proposing a solution.
  • No customer information is sold or published. Do not send sensitive data until a suitable written agreement and approved secure transfer process exist.
  • Messages are reviewed when attended; SRI does not claim continuous 24/7 human or automated response coverage.
