# Institutional discovery and supplier-registration roadmap

Snapshot: 22 November 2026. This is a planning register, not evidence that SRI is registered on any external portal.

## Why this is not a 195-country bulk signup

Supplier registration is generally organized by procurement network, legal entity and buyer eligibility—not one universal country-by-country form. A single qualified profile can expose a supplier to multiple institutions; it does not guarantee invitations, contracts or payments. Each directory and tender has its own rules. Never create duplicate or misleading profiles just to inflate country counts.

## Readiness documents to verify before registration

- Exact legal/constitution name of the supplier and whether SRI is a registered business, proprietorship, firm, company, trust or other eligible entity.
- Applicable business registration/licence number, date and issuing authority.
- Correct organization PAN / tax registration and any required GST status; never infer or invent.
- Official business address, phone, email and authorized representative.
- Bank account in the correct legal entity name; never publish bank details in this repository.
- Business activity description and procurement commodity/service codes mapped to actual deliverable capacity.
- References, prior contracts, financial statements, audited accounts, quality/security certifications only where actually held and required.
- Data protection, information security, subcontracting and conflict-of-interest declarations.
- Capability evidence: sample outputs, tested process, quality review records, delivery capacity, support terms and customer acceptance evidence.

## Safe sequence

1. Verify SRI's legal operating form and required documents with the owner.
2. Match each portal's requirements and relevant buying organizations to the current offer.
3. Prepare truthful profile copy and choose only codes that match demonstrated capability.
4. Owner reviews and submits all legal declarations, identity and banking details directly on official portal pages.
5. Record application date, portal reference, status, renewal/update dates and actual approval evidence. Do not label an application "registered" until the portal confirms it.
6. Monitor opportunities and participate only when eligibility, scope, capacity, deadline and data/security requirements are met.

## Outreach and directory rules

- Separate directory listing, supplier application, approved vendor status, tender submission, award, purchase order, payment, delivery and customer acceptance as distinct stages.
- Use public institutional/business contact routes only. Respect portal terms, tender Q&A rules, privacy notices, opt-outs and local laws.
- Do not scrape personal contact data, guess email addresses, bulk-message organizations or send unsolicited messages to procurement-only channels.
- No paid listing, registration agent, subscription or service purchase without separate cost/risk/benefit review and explicit owner approval.
